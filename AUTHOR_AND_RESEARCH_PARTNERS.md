# SYNTAVA — Authorship and Research-Partner Attribution
**Author clarification · 17 November 2026 · Applies to Scientific Publications I, II and the proposed III**

## Authorship and research programme

- **Sole scientific author of Publications I, II and the proposed III:** **John Kalyan Basu** (ORCID: [0009-0009-5561-4441](https://orcid.org/0009-0009-5561-4441)).
- **Interdisciplinary research programme:** **SYNTAVA**.
- **Research partners:** SYNTAVA is a multi-partner research programme. **THAL-KI (AI & Robotics), Switzerland, is one of its research partners, not its only research partner.** Other organisations are not named as partners without confirmation of their participation and the specific attribution appropriate to their contributions.
- **Not co-authorship:** Research partnership and affiliation do not confer scientific authorship. No institution or researcher cited in the bibliography becomes a SYNTAVA research partner merely by being cited.
- **Research independence:** The programme encompasses, but is not limited to, artificial intelligence and robotics. Its subject matter is interdisciplinary: neuroscience, consciousness science, neurotechnology and BCI, biotechnology and bioengineering, digital identity, artificial life and evolution, robotics, and computing and storage infrastructure.
