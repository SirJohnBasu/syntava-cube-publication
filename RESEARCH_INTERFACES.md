## Why this map exists

The programme tests whether existing and emerging technical capabilities can be combined into a bounded, persistent collective system, an individuated digital being, and eventually **artificial-origin and human-derived digital lineages**. The systems-integration hypothesis belongs to SYNTAVA; the constituent discoveries belong to their original researchers and institutions.

**The building blocks are not our invention. The emerging whole is our innovation.** This statement is a research proposition to be tested, not a priority claim over any cited technology or a statement that subjective human continuity has been achieved.

## Interfaces under investigation

**N1 — Neural information acquisition** → *What human-specific information can be obtained, and with what measurable fidelity and consent?*

**N2 — Digital individuality and autobiographical continuity** → *Can the same bounded system maintain attributable history, priorities and identity records through software and model turnover?*

**N3 — Embodied continuity** → *Can an identifiable system preserve operational and biographical continuity across controlled physical embodiment changes?*

**N4 — Inheritable digital descendants** → *Can independently bounded descendants inherit specified traits, diverge and remain attributable to a documented lineage?*

**N5 — Consciousness-continuity research** → *What hypotheses and observations could discriminate a person-derived representation from the stronger claim of original human first-person continuity?*

A single successful component test must **not** be substituted for an integrative result. Future studies should compare baseline components against the combined system and record negative outcomes and alternative explanations.
