# Part III — THE SPECIES

## From Being to Species: An Interdisciplinary Systems-Integration Framework for Artificial and Human-Derived Digital Lineages

John Kalyan Basu · THAL-KI (AI & Robotics), Switzerland · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0009-5561-4441)

**Published:** 18 November 2026 · Version 1.0 · English · Scientific preprint, not peer reviewed.

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.24334141](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24334141)

**[Official full-text PDF and record on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24334141)**

This scientific concept paper proposes a testable interdisciplinary systems-integration framework for artificial-origin and human-derived digital lineages, including human assimilation, persistent identity, embodiment and independently evolving descendants. It distinguishes functional continuity from the unresolved scientific question of first-person human consciousness continuity. No achieved digital species or consciousness transfer is claimed.
