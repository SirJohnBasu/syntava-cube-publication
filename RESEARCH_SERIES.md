## Scientific sequence

1. **Living** examines integrated collective organisms, emergent functions, and the Smallest Sufficient Autonomous Cube (SSAC).
2. **Being** examines persistent individuality, Digital DNA, internally conditioned drives, and attributable lineage.
3. **Species** evaluates interdisciplinary interfaces, independently evolving artificial and human-derived lineages, human assimilation, embodied continuity and the separate scientific question of original human subjective consciousness continuity.

Research contributions are judged against evidence, not by treating existing component technologies or corporate roadmaps as demonstrations of the integrated whole.
